A QUICK INTRODUCTION

Hey, I'm Rozette.

Founder, marketer and growth-minded creative.

Hi, I'm Rozette. I'm based in South Africa and I'm the founder of my Marketing Agency. I've been involved in both the Web2 and Web3 space for more than three years. Since entering these industries, I've developed a passion for helping NFT collections/projects, brands, businesses, projects, and companies grow through marketing, growth strategy, community building, and content creation. I'm also sharing my journey along the way, what I'm learning, the brands and businesses I work with, the challenges I face, and the lessons I gain from building in these spaces. I'm taking marketing and growth concepts and breaking them down into simple, practical explanations that people can understand and apply.

I'm curious by nature, always learning and interested in what makes brands, projects and communities move forward.
